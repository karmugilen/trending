Trending is a lightweight, zero-tracking, privacy-first personal developer radar.

Features:
• Instant Hacker News & GitHub breakout radar.
• Velocity metrics and frequency equalizer visualization.
• Emerging topic detection with lift analysis.
• Multi-ABI high performance native Rust engine paired with Jetpack Compose.
• Offline caching and privacy-first local operation (no external account or tracking).
• Security and vulnerability indicators.
